Fix `wp.DeterministicMode.RUN_TO_RUN` and `GPU_TO_GPU` having no effect on HIP devices, where kernels ran the plain atomic operations and results could differ between runs. Deterministic launches can also be captured in graphs on HIP. `RUN_TO_RUN` sums of `float16`, `bfloat16` and `float64` values are reduced in order on HIP, as in `GPU_TO_GPU`, so a very large sum into a single destination takes longer than on CUDA. No effect on CUDA devices or the CPU.
