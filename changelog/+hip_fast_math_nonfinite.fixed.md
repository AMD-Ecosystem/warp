Keep IEEE infinities and NaNs in `fast_math` modules on HIP. `-ffast-math` implies `-ffinite-math-only`, so `wp.isinf`, `wp.isfinite`, `wp.isnan` and comparisons with infinity gave wrong answers in fast-math kernels on HIP while `--use_fast_math` on CUDA keeps them; both HIP compile paths (HIPRTC and the out-of-process hipcc build) now pass `-fno-finite-math-only` after `-ffast-math`, and the hipcc build also gets the no-reassociation and flush-denormals corrections the HIPRTC path already had.
